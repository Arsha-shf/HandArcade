# Game guide

Rules, scoring and difficulty for each game. Press `ESC` in any game to return
to the menu.

## Fruit Slice

Swipe your **index fingertip** through flying fruit: apples, oranges,
watermelons, lemons, strawberries and kiwis. Each fruit splits into two cut
halves. Slicing two or more fruit in one swipe gives a combo bonus.

Choose **Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard** before each run.

| | Easy | Medium | Hard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lives | 5 | 5 | 3 |
| Sliced bomb costs a life | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Missed fruit costs a life | No | Yes | Yes |
| Speed | Slower | Normal | Faster |

Fruit launch in bursts that become faster and more frequent the longer you
survive. Bombs never appear in the first 5 seconds, and a burst never contains
more than one.

## Dodge

Your **palm** controls a small creature on screen. Asteroids, meteors and
crystals fly in from every edge, and spiky **mines** hunt you.

- Moving your hand closer to or farther from the camera changes your **depth**
  (shown on the gauge at the right edge).
- **Solid** obstacles are at your depth and can hurt you. **Faded** obstacles
  are at a different depth and pass through you safely.
- A red ring around a mine means it is still chasing you. Mines give up after a
  few seconds.
- Choose **Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard**. Easy has no mines. On Medium and Hard,
  swarms of mines are announced a moment before they arrive.

The game waits for your hand before it starts, and pauses if tracking loses it.
Nothing spawns in the first 1.5 seconds, and new obstacles are placed so you
always have time to react. Score is 10 points per second survived plus 10 points per
obstacle dodged. Each difficulty keeps its own best score.

## Catch

Swat falling objects with your **open palm**. Both hands work, and a fast swipe
catches everything along its path.

| Object | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Apple | 1 |
| Star | 3 |
| Gem | 5 (rare and fast) |
| Bomb | -2, and costs a miss |

Letting an apple, star or gem fall also costs a miss. Five misses end the run,
but **ten catches in a row heal one miss**. Bombs become more common as the game
goes on.

## Pinch Pop

You have 60 seconds. **Pinch your thumb and index finger together** on a bubble
to pop it. Both hands work.

| Bubble | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | +10 |
| Fast | +20; smaller and quicker |
| Golden | +60 |
| Chain | +25 and pops every non-bomb bubble nearby |
| Shield | +15 and blocks the next bomb you pop |
| Bomb | -15 and resets your combo |

Pops within 1.2 seconds of each other build a combo multiplier, up to x5. Every
so often a **Bubble Frenzy** starts, with more bubbles and double score. The round
gets harder as it goes: bubbles speed up and bombs become more common.
